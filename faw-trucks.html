FAW Trucks

Built tough. Priced right. Ready for the work Ghana demands.

FAW — First Automobile Works — is one of China's oldest and largest vehicle manufacturers, and their heavy truck division has become one of the most popular choices for commercial operators across Africa. There's a reason for that.

FAW trucks are built to work hard in conditions that would wear out lesser vehicles. They're cost-effective to buy, straightforward to maintain, and they've proven themselves on African roads time and time again. At Tratan Motors, we supply the FAW J6 and JH6 range — trucks that handle serious loads without drama.
