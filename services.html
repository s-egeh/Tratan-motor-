What We Do

More Than Just Trucks. Comprehensive Fleet Solutions.

Beyond supplying trucks and trailers, we support your business every step of the way.

We provide end-to-end support to ensure your transport operations run efficiently and profitably across Ghana.

Strategy, sourcing & ongoing support

Consultative support for fleet managers and owners — from first conversation to keeping your vehicles on the road.

Fleet consultation & strategy

Not sure which truck and trailer combination is best for your contracts? We offer expert consultation to analyze your payload requirements, routes, and budget, ensuring you invest in the exact machinery that maximizes your ROI.

Vehicle sourcing & procurement

Looking for a highly specific DAF or FAW configuration? We leverage our extensive industry network to source the exact heavy-duty vehicles you need, handling the logistics of getting them ready for Ghanaian roads.

Technical guidance & support

Our commitment doesn't end when you receive the keys. We provide ongoing technical advice, maintenance recommendations, and guidance on sourcing genuine parts to keep your fleet's downtime to an absolute minimum.

How we help

Everything we offer in one place — from supply and sourcing to trade-ins and after-sales care.

Truck Supply — DAF & FAW

We source and supply brand-new DAF and FAW trucks directly to businesses across Ghana. Whether you need one truck or a whole fleet, we handle the process from start to delivery.

Trailer Supply

We stock a range of semi-trailers — flatbeds, lowbeds, sidewalls, timber trailers — suitable for a wide variety of cargo and haulage operations across Ghana.

Fleet Consultation

Not sure what you need? We sit down with you, understand your operation, and recommend the right combination of trucks and trailers for your specific requirements — no guesswork.

Procurement & Sourcing

If you need something we don't have in stock, we can source it. Our manufacturer relationships mean we can get you what you need with shorter lead times than going direct.

Trade-In & Upgrade

Already running a fleet and looking to upgrade? Talk to us about trade-in options. We'll assess what you have and help you transition to newer, more efficient vehicles.

After-Sales Support

We don't disappear after the sale. If you have questions, need guidance, or run into issues, we're reachable. Call or email and someone will get back to you.
