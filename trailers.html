Trailers for Every Job

Whatever you're hauling — machinery, timber, bulk goods, oversized loads — we have the trailer that fits.

A truck is only as capable as the trailer behind it. That's why at Tratan Motors, we stock a range of semi-trailers designed to handle different cargo types and load requirements. Whether you need a basic flatbed for construction materials or a lowbed for heavy plant equipment, we've got options — and we'll help you match the right trailer to your operation.

Trailer Range

Flatbed Trailer

The flatbed is the most versatile trailer in the game. Open deck, easy to load from any side, and ideal for construction materials, steel, timber, and oversized goods that don't need enclosing.

Load capacity up to 40 tons · Multiple length options · Strong steel chassis · Twist locks available

Enquire

Lowbed / Lowloader Trailer

Lowbed trailers are designed for moving heavy machinery, excavators, and oversized equipment. The low deck height keeps your cargo within legal height limits even when the load is tall.

Up to 80 tons capacity · Hydraulic ramp option · Heavy-duty axle configuration · Wide deck available

Enquire

Sidewall / Side Board Trailer

Sidewall trailers give you the flexibility of a flatbed with the added containment of removable side panels. Great for loose cargo, agricultural goods, and bulk freight.

Removable side boards · High cargo volume · Multiple axle configurations · Durable flooring

Enquire

Timber / Log Trailer

Specifically designed for transporting timber and logs, these trailers have reinforced stanchions and securing systems to keep your load stable on long runs through rough terrain.

Heavy stanchion posts · Reinforced deck · Log bunks and chains · High weight tolerance
