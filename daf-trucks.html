DAF Trucks

European engineering. Built for long hauls, heavy loads, and Ghana's toughest routes.

DAF Trucks come out of the Netherlands and carry with them a reputation that's been built over decades. They're known across Europe and increasingly across Africa for being efficient on fuel, comfortable for drivers, and tough enough to keep running even when the conditions aren't ideal.

At Tratan Motors, we supply the New Generation DAF range — trucks designed from the ground up with both performance and driver wellbeing in mind. Whether you're running long-distance freight or regional distribution, DAF trucks are a sound investment.
