Who We Are

Tratan Motors has been in the business of supplying heavy-duty trucks and trailers to companies across Ghana for over a decade. We work directly with two of the world's most respected truck manufacturers — DAF Trucks from Europe and FAW Trucks from China — to bring reliable, high-performing vehicles to the Ghanaian market.

Whether you're running a mining operation, managing a logistics fleet, or growing a construction company, we understand that the right truck isn't just a purchase — it's a decision that affects your entire business. That's why we take time to understand what you actually need before recommending anything.

We're based in Ghana and we know these roads. We know the conditions, the loads, and what it takes for a truck to hold up over time. That local knowledge, combined with access to world-class vehicles, is what sets us apart.
